*This calculator is made for character creation and may not be fully accurate to real life.*

# Doggy-genetics
Doggy genetics is a dog coat color calculator that allows to create and manage dog characters ~~and family trees~~ [TBA].

It covers most of the currently known genes responsible for the colors and patterns of dog coats.
***
The *dog model* shows a simplified idea of what the dog may look like, not accounting for random factors (such as placement of merle spots or shape of white spotting) and differences in the fur type and length which may make the coat appear differently. 

The *Help window* tabs briefly describe the features and variety of each phenotype, to give some idea of how the result might range.
***

**Featured locii:**
- B locus (black and liver) and D locus (blue and isabella)
- I locus (simplified phaeomelanin intensity based on MFSD12)
- K locus (brindle)
- A locus (sable, saddle, agouti and tan)
- M locus (simplified merle) and H locus (Great Dane harlequin modifier)
- S locus (piebald white spotting)
- T locus (ticking and roan) and F locus (dalmatian flecking)

**Non-featured locii/alleles**:
- G locus (progressive greying) - only affects curly and wire-coated dogs, would not show on the model
- Advanced merle genetics on the M locus - hard to predict all the different combinations
- Northern (ancient) domino, grizzle (sighthound domino) and cocker sable on the E locus - hard to predict all the different combinations
- Co locus (French Bulldog cocoa) - hard to predict all the different combinations
Once there are new dog genetics discoveries, I will be happy to add them in.
